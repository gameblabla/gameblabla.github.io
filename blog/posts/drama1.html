So a quick recap.

Back in Late january of 2024, i had the idea of coming up with a quick visual novel for a game for the NEC PC-FX.

At the time, i had made a few tests with software rendering but concluded that without more knowledge of the system, that i couldn’t make games that would look better than either a PC Engine game or be very slow (as demonstrated by my port of nCraft or Worship Vector).

I was thinking of a few characters before i settled on these two because they looked very similar and could be easily generated.

Some of you had suspicious but yes, the pictures were AI generated with Stable Diffusion 1.5 with a custom LORA. Looking back at it, i can’t help but cringe at the quality of these but somehow this was good enough to fool people.

I made up a plot that was similar to games that you would typically find on the PC-98 and PC-FX (and yes, some of these games do include sexual assault), except that i would skip portions like Mahjong or whatever, because quite frankly, no one likes to have the sex scenes locked behind a difficult game of Mahjong or whatever.

Anyway, i will spare you the technical details about actually making the game because for the most part it’s nothing special (except for the fact there’s few PC-FX homebrew games) but i did learn a lot about the system itself.

Besides, people on the PC-FX and PC-98 are playing games with little girls in diapers (See Chip Chan Kick on PC-FX), or even games that allow you to rape your enemies after defeating them, surely they would tolerate that was more tame like this, right ?
